What your organization gets

Covering the moments staff cannot reach

Care staff go off shift; caring does not have to. Here is what residents, families and your organization each get.

Voice companionship that remembers

Residents just talk, with no typing. Long-term memory lets each conversation pick up where the last one left off, and someone is there to talk to at night and in thinly staffed hours.

Medication and appointment reminders

Residents get a phone notification on time, and a missed dose is never scolded. Munea only reminds; it does not assess or change medication.

Families can see updates

Families connect on their own phones and see the updates, mood and reminder status a resident chooses to share. What they can see is up to the resident.

Monthly operations report

Organization seats include a monthly report covering usage, care and alert summaries, so managers can see what is actually happening after rollout.

How to get started

  1. Pick 10–20 residents for a 2–4 week pilot, and review each week how smoothly it runs, what families say and when staff need to step in.
  2. Seat-based, pay for what you use: each seat includes a fixed monthly allowance of call credits (Plus 100, Pro 200; 1 credit is about 1 minute), with top-ups available.
  3. Decide to expand, adjust or stop against goals agreed up front; final pricing and responsibilities are set in the contract.
