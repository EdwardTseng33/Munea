Information we process

Depending on the features you use, Munea processes the following information:

  • Account and sign-in: the email address provided when you sign in with Apple or Google.
  • About you and your family: your name or preferred form of address, birth month and year, and city, plus the names and relationships of the people in your family circle.
  • Reminders and settings: companion and interest settings, medication and appointment reminders, and emergency contacts.
  • Health numbers: steps, heart rate, blood pressure (systolic and diastolic), blood oxygen, and sleep, from Apple Health when you allow it or entered by you, plus your medication records.
  • Mood: mood entries you record, and mood the AI derives from your chats.
  • Chats and family interaction: what you say and type, AI-generated key points of your chats, and family messages, notes, drawings, and check-ins.
  • Safety notification records: records created when a health number looks off or something in a chat sounds worrying.
  • Usage records: which features you use and whether calls connect, used to fix problems and improve the product.
  • Purchase information: your plan and credits. Apple processes payment; Munea does not receive your payment-card number.

Where AI and cloud processing happens, including other countries

Munea companions are AI. The face and voice you experience in Chat are AI-generated virtual imagery and synthetic speech, not a real person, and are not used to impersonate any real person.

These are the service providers we use and what each one receives. Some of them are outside your country or region:

  • Google’s AI (Gemini): what you say and type is sent to Google’s AI for processing. Google decides where processing happens, and it may be outside your country. During a call, your health numbers and mood are also given to the AI for reference, and the AI derives key points and your mood from your chats. When the AI needs to look something up during a chat, it uses Google Search. We use Google’s paid service: under the Gemini API terms (version effective March 23, 2026), Google does not use this content to improve its products. Google keeps content for a limited period to prevent abuse, maintain safety, and meet legal requirements.
  • Supabase (database): your account, family circle, reminders, health numbers, mood, and chat key points are stored in Supabase’s cloud database, hosted in Tokyo, Japan.
  • Google Cloud (hosting): Munea’s backend runs in Google Cloud’s Taiwan region.
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, payment and subscriptions, and push notifications.
  • Google Sign-In: used when you choose to sign in with a Google account.
  • Slack (internal team notifications): service alerts, the level and category of safety notifications, and the first few words of feedback you send from the App go to the team’s internal notification channel. Chat content and health numbers are not included.
  • Public weather and place data sources (Open-Meteo, Taiwan’s Central Weather Administration and Ministry of Environment, OpenStreetMap): when looking up weather, air quality, or nearby places, only the city is sent, without your name or account.

We do not store recordings ourselves; we keep only the key points of your chats. Your voice is not used to identify you (no voiceprint recognition).

Before you first use Chat, the App shows a notice, and Chat starts only after you tap the button to continue. If you do not agree, you cannot use Chat; reminders and family features still work. To withdraw consent, the current way is to delete your account or email us and ask us to stop processing.

This information is used only for companionship, reminders, safety notifications, and improving the product. We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Who can see what

  • Every family member in your family circle: can see your health numbers, your mood (including mood the AI derives from your chats), and your medication records. They cannot see what you said in chats, or medication photos, which stay only on your phone. There is currently no setting to share with only some family members: everyone who joins your family circle can see these.
  • The emergency contacts you choose: receive a safety notification when a health number looks off or something in a chat sounds worrying. The notification does not include chat content.
  • Google’s AI: what you say and type, plus the health numbers and mood given to the AI during a call (see the section above).
  • Partner organizations or companies (only if your account is provided by a care organization or a company): a company contact sees only totals for the whole group. Staff at a care organization can see that you are on their list, attention flags, and a weekly care summary only after your consent has been recorded. They cannot see chat content.
  • The Munea team: may look at your account information and records when needed for support, handling safety notifications, and fixing problems.

If you set Munea up for a family member (for example, installing it and signing in for a parent): first tell them what information Munea processes and who can see it, and let them know they can delete it at any time.

Apple Health and HealthKit information

After you give permission, Munea reads these six items through Apple HealthKit: steps, heart rate, blood pressure (systolic and diastolic), blood oxygen, and sleep. Munea only reads; it does not write.

  • How it is used: in-App status, trends, and reminders; as reference for the AI during a call; visible to family members in your family circle; and to notify the emergency contacts you choose when a number looks off.
  • Never used for advertising or marketing: health information is not sold, and is not given to advertisers, data brokers, or third parties unrelated to care features.
  • Storage: the health numbers Munea reads are stored in our cloud database (Tokyo, Japan). Munea does not write HealthKit data to iCloud.
  • You can turn it off anytime: you can turn off Munea’s Health access in iPhone Settings. Other App features still work if you never grant access or later turn it off.
  • Deletion: deleting your account deletes the data in your account. Health records you left in someone else’s family circle are not deleted with it; email us and we will delete them for you.

Location and photos

Location: Munea uses only the city you enter (stored in the cloud with your profile) to look up weather, air quality, and nearby places. We do not collect precise GPS location.

Medication photos: photos you attach to medication reminders are stored only on your phone. They are not uploaded, and family members cannot see them.

No advertising tracking

Munea does not use advertising or cross-app tracking technologies, does not link information to your identity for advertising, and does not give your information to data brokers or advertisers.

How long we keep information

  • Your information is kept until you delete it or delete your account. If the retention rules change, we will tell you here and in the App first.
  • Recordings: we do not store recordings. Google keeps content for a limited period under its own terms (see “Where AI and cloud processing happens” above).
  • Chat history: in the App you can look back at records from the past year.
  • Some payment, safety, or legally required records may need to be kept longer.

Health and safety limits

Munea is a companionship and daily-reminder tool. It does not provide diagnosis, prescriptions, treatment advice, psychotherapy, or emergency response, and it is not a medical device.

Safety notifications: when a health number looks off or something in a chat sounds worrying, Munea records it and notifies the emergency contacts you choose. This is not a real-time alert. For urgent symptoms, danger, or risk of self-harm, contact your local emergency service or a trusted person nearby immediately.

Minors

Munea is not offered to people under 18 to register on their own. The App does not currently check age; if we find that a minor has registered on their own, we will delete the account. A minor who wants to use Munea needs a parent or legal guardian to agree and help with setup.

Your data rights

In the App, under Settings → My data, you can download a copy of your data or delete your account.

  • The downloaded copy does not yet include health records, your medication list, or emergency contacts. If you need these, email us and we will provide them separately.
  • Deleting your account deletes the data on your phone and in your account, except limited payment, audit, safety, or legally required records that may need to be kept longer. Health records you left in someone else’s family circle are not deleted with it; email us and we will delete them for you.
  • To stop processing or withdraw consent: the current way is to delete your account or email us and ask us to stop.
  • You can also email us at any time to request access, correction, or deletion.

Contact us

For questions about data processing, or to exercise the rights above, email edwardt0303@gmail.com or contact the Munea team through App Settings.
